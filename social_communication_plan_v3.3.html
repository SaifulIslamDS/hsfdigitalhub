Communication Philosophy

HSF communication should connect a real issue with a credible response, verified evidence and an appropriate next action.

Open detailed reference
Professional but cordial. Heart-touching but never exploitative. Thoughtful but practical. Evidence-led without losing humanity — designed to move empathy toward responsible action.

The framework remains open-ended. Themes are reusable communication lanes rather than weeks or fixed calendar blocks.

Core communication sequence

Issue → Response → Evidence → Engagement → Learning → Better Communication

Each of the 24 themes contains its own connected Issue post and Resolution post so awareness and usefulness remain together. The first 48 posts should be treated as one pilot cycle rather than divided into separate issue and response halves.

Operating guardrails

  • Pity should not be used as the main persuasion mechanism.
  • Communities should not be portrayed as passive recipients and HSF should not be portrayed as a rescuer.
  • Practical guidance should be source-backed and technically reviewed where the subject requires specialist knowledge.
  • Project evidence should be described at the strength supported by the records.
  • One clear primary action should normally be associated with each content item.
  • Publishing frequency should remain subordinate to evidence quality, consent, approval and production readiness.

SDG and Rights Framework

SDGs and rights lenses provide context; they should not replace the human issue, project evidence or practical message.

Open detailed reference

Relevant HSF communication may reference SDG 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 11, 13, 16 and 17, together with applicable child-rights and human-rights principles. A rights or SDG label should be included only when it helps the audience understand the issue.

De-duplication rule: Detailed evidence definitions remain in the Impact, MEAL & Learning Framework → Evidence Hierarchy. Website publishing requirements remain in the Website Content & Editorial Guidelines.

Daily Communication Operating Model

v3.3 establishes two distinct daily communication streams. The morning shows HSF's real work and institutional life when meaningful content exists; the afternoon protects the locked P01–P48 strategic sequence.

Open the 48-day operating model
Morning — HSF in Action: normally around 9:00–10:00 AM; FIELD / MOMENT / EVENT / OBS / HSF when relevant. Afternoon — HSF Knowledge & Awareness: normally around 4:00–5:00 PM; one locked P01–P48 core post per day.
Morning · HSF in Action

Real activity, people and institutional life

  • The morning slot is activity- and relevance-led, not quota-led.
  • Use FIELD, MOMENT, EVENT, OBS or HSF according to the communication role.
  • Authentic photography and concise human context should normally lead routine FIELD/MOMENT communication.
  • If the planned activity did not occur, evidence is weak, safeguarding is unresolved, or the content would be repetitive, replace or skip the slot.
Afternoon · HSF Knowledge & Awareness

One core post every day

  • Publish one P01–P48 core post around 4:00–5:00 PM.
  • Issue today → Resolution tomorrow.
  • Each theme therefore occupies two consecutive core-publishing days.
  • 24 themes × 2 days = one 48-day core cycle.
  • The 3 Standard + 1 Special Engagement rhythm remains a format rule, not a frequency rule.

Protected pair rule

The morning overlay does not interrupt the afternoon core sequence. An observance or field post can appear in the morning while the afternoon Issue → Resolution pair continues normally across consecutive days.

Current-cycle control

The cycle beginning 1 September 2026 started with FIELD-001 — E4BL Hazaribagh Learning Material Distribution in the morning and P01 — When Money Becomes a Barrier to Learning in the afternoon. P02 is the next core post. Day-to-day status belongs in communication/registers/HSF_Social_Communication_48-Day_Cycle_v3.3_Tracker.xlsx; the policy HTML should not be edited after every publication.

Morning decision gate

Is it real? → Is it useful? → Is it safe? → Is it sufficiently different? → Is evidence available? → Is publication better than silence?

3+1 Publishing Rhythm

The 3+1 rhythm controls format variety across the 48-day P01–P48 sequence. It does not change the one-core-post-per-day cadence.

Open publishing logic
Every theme = 2 posts: Issue → Resolution. Across the full sequence: three standard-format posts are followed by one Special Engagement post.
Theme logic

Issue → Resolution

The Issue post should create awareness and understanding. The Resolution post should provide a practical, verified pathway, guidance, remedy, protective action or responsible next step.

Format rhythm

3 Standard + 1 Special

Posts 04, 08, 12 and every fourth post thereafter should normally use a richer format such as a Reel, short video, expert voice, Q&A, demonstration, animated Myth vs Fact or community challenge.

Post sequenceCommunication roleFormat treatment
01IssueStandard
02ResolutionStandard
03IssueStandard
04ResolutionSpecial Engagement
The same rhythm continues through Post 48.

Special Engagement does not mean extra. A Special Engagement item remains one of the 48 core posts; only its presentation format changes. The separate HSF in Action — Special Overlay, introduced in v3.2 and operationally formalized in v3.3, is different: it normally occupies the morning communication window and never renumbers or replaces the afternoon core sequence.

Version control: v3.3 is the stable operating baseline effective 1 September 2026. Previous stable communication baseline: v3.2 (22 August 2026). Day-to-day publication status is maintained in the 48-day tracker rather than in this policy page.

First 48-Post Plan

The first operational library is intentionally simple: 24 themes, one Issue post and one Resolution post under each theme.

Open plan logic
Theme 01 → Issue 01 → Resolution 02 → Theme 02 → Issue 03 → Resolution 04 (Special) → continue through Theme 24 → Post 48
  • Each post should have one primary communication purpose and a clearly identified audience.
  • HSF evidence may appear in either the Issue or Resolution post when verified and relevant; a separate evidence post is not required.
  • Every fourth post should normally receive a Special Engagement treatment without increasing the total post count.
  • Technical advice should remain source-backed and professionally reviewed where necessary.
  • The first 48 posts should be treated as the first cycle. Performance and learning should shape the next cycle.

Observance selection rule

HSF should not post for every international or national day. An observance should normally be selected only when it has a credible relationship to an HSF programme, one or more of the 24 themes, an SDG/right, verified HSF evidence or a clear institutional value.

Publish when
  • The day is directly relevant to HSF work or values.
  • HSF can add useful context, evidence, reflection or a practical action.
  • The official date/theme/source has been checked for the current year.
  • The content can be produced with dignity, accuracy and appropriate approval.
Skip when
  • The connection to HSF is weak or artificial.
  • The post would be only a generic greeting card.
  • The day would interrupt a stronger programme story without adding value.

Education journey and CSI accuracy

  • HSF's own school / learning-centre capacity currently extends through Grade 5.
  • A student who completes Grade 5 from the HSF school / learning centre is referred to as an HSF Graduate.
  • Eligible HSF Graduates may continue in independent third-party secondary schools with support through the Child Sponsorship Initiative (CSI).
  • Under the current CSI model, an individual donor supports an individual CSI student, while HSF manages the sponsorship fund.
  • HSF teachers are assigned to supervise groups / clusters of CSI students and support appropriate monitoring and follow-up.
  • Public wording should distinguish HSF's support role from the role of the independent secondary school. HSF should not imply that it operates the student's secondary school.
Grade 1 entry → Grade 5 completion → HSF Graduate → Third-party secondary-school transition → CSI support → Current verified status → Future aspiration

Safeguarding, Privacy and Accuracy

Dignity, consent, privacy, source quality and claim accuracy remain non-negotiable.

Open detailed reference
  • An identifiable child story should not be published without appropriate permission, age-appropriate participation and safeguarding review.
  • Consent to receive an HSF service should not automatically be treated as consent for public storytelling, photography, video or reuse on other platforms.
  • Patient records, prescriptions, diagnoses, addresses, phone numbers and confidential health information should remain private.
  • Where risk warrants it, a story may use first name only, an explicitly disclosed changed name, non-identifying photography, voice-only treatment or other privacy-preserving presentation.
  • Humiliating poverty imagery, exaggerated hardship, staged distress and rescuer narratives should not be used.
  • AI-generated people should not be presented as actual HSF participants or beneficiaries.
  • A statistic, quotation, timeline, health outcome or educational status should not be invented or strengthened beyond the available evidence.
  • A patient consultation should not be represented as proof of treatment or recovery unless the stronger claim is independently supported and approved.
  • Partner logos should be used only with authorization.
  • Advice-oriented content should remain inside the competence and review boundaries defined under Verified Community Guidance.
Detailed responsible-data, evidence and qualitative-story controls are maintained in the Impact, MEAL & Learning Framework.

Implementation and Board Review

The framework should be treated as a living institutional system that can be refined after management discussion and real publishing experience.

Open implementation sequence
  1. Board/management confirmation that the 24-theme, 48-post Issue–Resolution structure remains the fixed core cycle.
  2. Confirmation of technical reviewers and approval responsibilities for advice-oriented content.
  3. Creation of a rolling Special Communication Overlay register for OBS, HSF, FIELD, EVENT and MOMENT items, including relevant annual observances and internal HSF milestones.
  4. Production of Posts 01–48 using verified current project inputs and approved external sources while protecting each Issue–Resolution pair.
  5. Production of overlay posts only when relevance, evidence, timing, safeguarding and approval requirements are met.
  6. Application of the Brand Identity Master Guide to core, Special Engagement and overlay formats, including albums, carousels and short-form motion.
  7. Measurement and learning reported separately for the 48 core posts and the special overlay stream.
  8. Development of the next core content cycle only after the first cycle has generated useful evidence and learning; the overlay may continue responsively throughout the cycle.
The Knowledge Hub itself is expected to grow. Additional policies, SOPs, training resources, research, benchmark studies and planning documents may be added as they become institutionally useful and approved.

Final operating principle

HSF social communication should not merely tell people what HSF does; it should help people understand important challenges, discover credible and practical responses, see verified human and project evidence, and move from awareness and empathy toward responsible action.
Simple planning model
  1. 1. Issue post
  2. 2. Resolution post
  3. 3+1. Every 4th core post = Special format
  4. + Timely OBS / HSF / FIELD / EVENT / MOMENT overlay
Quality rule

Relevance, evidence, dignity, usefulness and approval should determine publication. The 3+1 rhythm remains a core-format pattern; the special overlay is responsive and never a publishing quota.
