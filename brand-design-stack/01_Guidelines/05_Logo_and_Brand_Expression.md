# Logo & Brand Expression Governance

## Logo
There is one authoritative HSF vector logo source. Do not trace, redraw, recolour or create per-template logo copies. Masters contain `LOGO_COMPONENT_SLOT` only.

## Brand expressions
- Evergreen — default master identity
- Trust Blue — trust, health systems, governance, partnerships
- Hope Gold — education, youth, opportunity, participation
- Care Red — urgency, protection, crisis/action
- Dignity Violet — inclusion, empowerment, safeguarding

Use `06_Tools/recolor_svg.py` to create a recoloured working copy while retaining the single template master.
