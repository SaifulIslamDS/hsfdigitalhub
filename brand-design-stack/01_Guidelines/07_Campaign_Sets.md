# Campaign Sets

A campaign set is one communication idea adapted natively across platforms, not one resized artwork.

Example — International Literacy Day (OBS):
- Facebook 4:5 observance post
- Instagram 4:5 observance post
- Instagram 3:4 photo / Story / Reel cover as needed
- LinkedIn 4:5 institutional evidence post or document carousel

Example — HSF Foundation Day (HSF):
- Facebook Page/event post + album cover
- Instagram institutional-memory carousel + Reel cover
- LinkedIn milestone/evidence post or document carousel
