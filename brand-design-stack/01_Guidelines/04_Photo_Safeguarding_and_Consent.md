# Photo, Safeguarding & Consent

Before placing any real photograph:
- confirm consent/authorization where required;
- protect children, patients and vulnerable people;
- remove unnecessary identifiers;
- do not show registers, prescriptions, medical records, IDs, phone numbers or private addresses;
- inspect backgrounds and screens;
- avoid exploitative poverty/suffering framing;
- prefer activity/group imagery where safer;
- verify captions and claims;
- remove sensitive location/metadata where appropriate.

Human-centred communication must never become exploitative communication.
