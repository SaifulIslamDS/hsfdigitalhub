# Platform-Native Rules

## Facebook
Community-oriented, accessible, field/event/photo friendly. Medium information density. Protect responsive Page-cover crops and profile overlap.

## Instagram
Visual-first, human and photo-led. Use less text, stronger hierarchy, carousels and Reels. 3:4 is available for photography; 4:5 remains the HSF campaign/feed master.

## LinkedIn
Institutional, evidence-led, professional. More room for findings, partnership, learning and publication content. Use document posts/PDF carousels for knowledge-heavy stories.

## Mobile legibility
Every format, not only 9:16, must be readable on a phone without zooming.
