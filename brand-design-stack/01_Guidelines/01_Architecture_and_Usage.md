# Architecture & Usage

## Production decision flow
1. Choose platform: Facebook / Instagram / LinkedIn.
2. Choose communication stream: Core or Overlay.
3. Choose content family / event role.
4. Choose platform-native format.
5. Choose brand expression according to programme/topic.
6. Replace photo placeholders only with approved, consented HSF media.
7. Insert the approved central logo master into `LOGO_COMPONENT_SLOT`.
8. Add only verified evidence and sources.
9. Run mobile-legibility and safeguarding checks.
10. Export the platform-appropriate PNG/JPG/PDF/video.

## Adaptive density
Facebook = medium information density. Instagram = low/visual-first. LinkedIn = higher institutional/evidence density. Do not merely resize one platform's artwork into another platform.
