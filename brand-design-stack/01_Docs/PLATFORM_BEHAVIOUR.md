# Platform Behaviour

## Facebook
Community-oriented. Medium information density. Strong for event documentation, albums, FIELD posts and accessible human stories.

## Instagram
Most photo-led. Low-to-medium information density. Prioritize people, image hierarchy, carousel storytelling, Stories and Reels.

## LinkedIn
Institutional and evidence-led. Medium-to-high information density is acceptable, especially in PDF documents, reports and presentations. Photography should still lead human-centred stories.

## Siblings, not clones
A campaign can share one story identity while layouts adapt to each platform. Never simply resize one platform's artwork three times.
