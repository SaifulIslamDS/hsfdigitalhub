# Brand Stack Governance Alignment Note

**Document review:** Stable reference · **Last updated:** 20 October 2026 · **Planning & Compiled by:** Saiful Islam

**Updated:** 11 September 2026

The Brand Design Stack v3.1.0 was originally built against Social Communication Plan v3.2 and Brand Identity & Social Design System v2.2. The Knowledge Hub later promoted Social v4.0 and Brand v2.4.

The stack metadata is now aligned to those current stable governance versions. The production assets themselves remain **v3.1.0 production candidate** and are not silently promoted to a new stable stack release.

The image-first storytelling rules, platform adaptation logic, campaign-set concepts, focal-point/crop governance and SVG-master approach remain valid design guidance where they do not conflict with the current stable Social v4.0 / Brand v2.4 documents.

## Remaining certification step

Representative production SVGs should be opened and checked in Adobe Illustrator 2022 for:

- editability;
- font/text handling;
- clipping/masking;
- linked/embedded image behavior;
- logo replacement workflow;
- platform export quality.

Until that QA is documented, retain the status **production candidate**.
