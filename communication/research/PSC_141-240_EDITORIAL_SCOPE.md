Each idea contains a particular conditional angle, original field-evidence requirement, appropriate output format, safeguarding/technical risk, blank source reference, `NOT_CONFIRMED` occurrence and `NOT_VERIFIED` fact state. The author must first establish that the actual occurrence or approved institutional action exists.

The [`100-idea originality review`](PSC_141-240_UNIQUENESS_REVIEW.json) records nearby older titles/angles from 400 previous ideas plus the distinct required documentation. That is a screening aid only. Management/Communication reviewers may merge, reject or revise any proposal after comparing underlying story routes and field availability.

**PSC is field-evidence-led.** Source-led climate literacy and medical/education explainers belong in KDI, not disguised as HSF field events. Never invent a beneficiary, quote, impact result, referral, clinical action, event, partnership or consent.

After HSF idea approval, work must still go through: occurrence record → source/MEAL verification → technical/clinical check as applicable → safeguarding/permission → reviewed exact caption + media → management-approved publication record.
