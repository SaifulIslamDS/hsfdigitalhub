## Overlap review protocol

1. Review **all 300 previous** KDI/PSC topics by title, focus and expected learning outcome before accepting a new idea.
2. Check `KDI_161-260_UNIQUENESS_REVIEW.json` for the five nearest titles; use it as editorial review evidence, **not proof of zero semantic overlap**.
3. A narrower concept is acceptable only when it answers a materially different question with its own evidence needs and audience learning outcome.
4. If further editing identifies duplicates, merge or replace the proposed idea **before editorial approval**, while retaining its original ID and versioned audit trail.
5. No generic "climate change is real" post should displace existing evidence-led KDI-071..100, KDI-121..134 or KDI-148..155.

## Publication gates

Climate: exact source, reporting period, geography, scientific uncertainty and civil-society vs official position. Education: pedagogy/age/grade technical review. Healthcare: licensed clinical review, local clinical guidance, no personalized diagnosis or prescription. All: safeguarding, brand checks, source verification and final management approval of the exact public output.
