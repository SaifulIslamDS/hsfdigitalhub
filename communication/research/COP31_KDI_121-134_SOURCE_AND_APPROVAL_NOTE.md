## Distinguishing types of claims

1. The booklet’s policy priorities are attributable to its civil-society authors; do not describe them as formally adopted Bangladesh Government priorities without an official source.
2. A national NDC target is different from an achieved outcome; label projected pathways and conditional commitments accurately.
3. Finance pledges are different from receipts and actual disbursements.
4. Climate harm, gender, public-health, education, livelihoods and WASH statistics must use dated, traceable original publications, with reporting scope and uncertainty.
5. COP31 deliberations and negotiated outcomes must not be implied to have occurred unless confirmed after the event by authoritative sources.
