## Canonical codes and display terminology (v1.8.3 alignment)

The Digital System Contract v1.0.0 controls internal codes and canonical names. Its `CLIMATE_ACTION` canonical name remains **Climate Action**. The approved public-facing full display name is **Climate Action & Resilience**; **Climate Action** may appear after the first mention or as a short label. This is a presentation alias, **not** a new programme or contract revision.

| Code | Contract canonical name | Full public display name | Acceptable short label |
|---|---|---|---|
| `E4BL` | Education for Better Life | Education for Better Life (E4BL) | E4BL |
| `A2PHC` | Access to Primary Healthcare | Access to Primary Healthcare (A2PHC) | A2PHC |
| `CLIMATE_ACTION` | Climate Action | Climate Action & Resilience | Climate Action |

In public-facing HSF copy, use **Project** for the three operational delivery areas, consistent with Website Editorial Guidelines. Internal databases, governance records, programme codes and historical document titles may continue using **programme** where appropriate. Do not rename schema fields or historical records for display purposes.

Women and girls' empowerment, safeguarding and inclusion are **cross-cutting topics**, not separately activated fourth Projects. Historical plans and unapproved ERP/UI mock-ups must be labelled as historical or optional.

## Unconfirmed / blueprint domains

`WOMEN_EMPOWERMENT` may exist in ERP/UI/product blueprints, but its presence in a design does not establish an active HSF programme. Management confirmation is required before it is treated as a current programme master.

## Organizational dimensions

Central Office Management/COM is an organizational and financial cost-centre dimension, not automatically a programme.

## Rule for systems and reports

Programme names/codes in ERP, MEAL, reports and public communication should use this registry unless a controlled management-approved update supersedes it.
