# HSF Programme Registry

**Status:** Canonical institutional programme registry  
**Effective:** 11 September 2026

| Code | Programme | Status | Institutional domain |
|---|---|---|---|
| `E4BL` | Education for Better Life | Current programme | Education continuity |
| `A2PHC` | Access to Primary Healthcare | Current programme | Community-based primary healthcare |
| `CLIMATE_ACTION` | Climate Action | Current programme | Climate awareness, youth/community engagement and related climate-action work |

## Unconfirmed / blueprint domains

`WOMEN_EMPOWERMENT` may exist in ERP/UI/product blueprints, but its presence in a design does not establish an active HSF programme. Management confirmation is required before it is treated as a current programme master.

## Organizational dimensions

Central Office Management/COM is an organizational and financial cost-centre dimension, not automatically a programme.

## Rule for systems and reports

Programme names/codes in ERP, MEAL, reports and public communication should use this registry unless a controlled management-approved update supersedes it.
