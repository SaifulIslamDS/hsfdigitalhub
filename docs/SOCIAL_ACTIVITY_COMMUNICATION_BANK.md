# HSF Activity Communication Bank

**Status:** Controlled planning reference  
**Governing principle:** Activity planning does not equal evidence of occurrence.

## Source hierarchy

1. **HSF Regular Activity List** — primary planning source for recurring, ongoing, seasonal and as-needed activity.
2. **HSF Detailed Activity Inventory** — supporting evidence/history catalogue.
3. **Current verified programme record** — final publication authority.

## Planning groups

### E4BL — Education & Child Development
Potential communication subjects include regular classroom teaching, learning materials, guardian communication, progression support, hygiene, nutrition where actually provided, creative/cultural activity, sports and seasonal activities.

### A2PHC — Primary Healthcare
Potential subjects include verified medical camps, MBBS consultation environments, community/yard meetings, health literacy, nutrition awareness, menstrual-health education and other approved awareness activity.

**Current boundary:** medicine is not a standard current service claim unless the specific activity record verifies it.

### Climate Action
Potential subjects include youth climate dialogue, resilience discussions, environmental awareness, tree planting/care, climate-friendly agriculture and verified community engagement.

### Women, Girls, Children & Rights
Potential subjects include girls' education/participation, rights awareness, dignity, inclusion and verified protection-related awareness.

### Humanitarian / Social Protection
Potential subjects include winter support, Eid support, Iftar/social inclusion and disaster/emergency assistance only when current, timely and verified.

### Livelihood / Volunteer / Institutional
Potential subjects include farmer/livelihood support, volunteers at work, partnerships, coordination/learning meetings, publications and institutional milestones.

## Publication workflow

**Plan → Verify occurrence → Evidence/media check → Safeguarding/privacy → Technical/factual review if needed → Approval → Publish → Track → Learn**

## Activity classification

Where useful, classify source activity as:

- Current regular
- Seasonal
- As needed
- Historical
- Requires current verification

Do not silently upgrade historical or uncertain material to current operational status.
