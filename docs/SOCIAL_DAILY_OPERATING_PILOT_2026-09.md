# HSF Social Communication — Daily Operating Pilot

**Document ID:** HSF-COM-PILOT-2026-09  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** 1 September 2026  
**Status:** Draft/Pilot  
**Classification:** Internal / institutional reference  
**Related stable documents:** Social Communication Plan v3.2; Brand Identity & Social Design System v2.2

## 1. Purpose

This pilot tests a two-window daily publishing model while protecting the locked P01–P48 core sequence.

The pilot is an operational test, not a silent amendment of the stable v3.2 baseline.

## 2. Daily publishing model

| Window | Stream | Working label | Purpose | Typical treatment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9:00–10:00 AM | FIELD / MOMENT / EVENT; OBS/HSF where date or milestone requires | **HSF in Action** | Show what HSF is actually doing | authentic, photo-led, short, human |
| 4:00 PM | P01 → P48 | **HSF Knowledge & Awareness** | Strategic education, health, climate, rights and community communication | planned, designed, evidence-led |

### Protected rule

**4:00 PM is reserved for the locked core series.**

A field activity, event, observance or organizational moment must not renumber, replace or displace P01–P48.

## 3. Morning stream hierarchy

### MOMENT — frequent/light

Use when one authentic image communicates a meaningful human or organizational moment.

Typical treatment: **1 strong photo + 2–4 short sentences.**

No statistic or CTA is required when human connection or understanding is enough.

### FIELD — meaningful programme activity

Use for real implementation activity such as E4BL classroom work, examinations, learning-material distribution, teacher/guardian engagement, A2PHC health camps or awareness activity, and Climate Action/youth engagement.

Typical treatment: **2–5 photos + concise narrative.**

Preferred order:

**What happened → Why it mattered → Who participated → What was achieved or learned**

Only state results that the evidence supports.

### EVENT — substantial activity

Use for events that justify structured documentation or storytelling.

- **Album = Documentation**
- **Carousel = Storytelling**
- Reel/video only where the event significance and available media justify it.

### OBS / HSF — date- or milestone-led

Use when a relevant observance, Foundation Day or institutional milestone has a genuine HSF programme, rights, SDG or institutional connection.

## 4. Opportunity-led, not quota-led

A morning communication window exists, but HSF is **not required to fill it every day**.

Overlay publishing is activity- and relevance-led. A post should be published only when there is meaningful, safe, evidence-supported content with a clear communication purpose.

An empty calendar slot is preferable to repetitive or unsupported content.

## 5. Morning publishing decision gate

Before drafting or approving a morning post, ask:

1. **Is it real?** — Is the activity/event/moment actually documented?
2. **Is it useful?** — Does the audience gain understanding, connection, information or institutional context?
3. **Is it safe?** — Have consent, safeguarding and privacy been checked?
4. **Is it sufficiently different?** — Does it add value beyond recent posts from the same activity/programme?
5. **Is evidence available?** — Can factual claims be traced to an appropriate source?
6. **Is publication better than silence?** — If not, do not post.

## 6. Frequency safeguards

- Normally publish no more than **one morning overlay**.
- Do not split one routine activity into multiple posts merely to increase frequency.
- One major EVENT may generate more than one item only when each item has a different communication purpose.
- Avoid over-representing the programme with the easiest photographs.
- Keep the morning post materially shorter than the 4 PM core post.
- Protect the core Issue → Resolution relationship even when overlays are active.

## 7. Programme balance

Track morning communication across:

- E4BL
- A2PHC
- Climate Action
- HSF institutional/milestone content
- other approved HSF programmes or cross-cutting themes

Programme balance is a management signal, not an equal-quota requirement.

## 8. Evidence boundary

**Activity ≠ output ≠ outcome ≠ impact.**

Examples:

- “Students received learning materials” may be supported by an activity record and approved photographs.
- “The materials improved attendance” requires separate attendance evidence.
- “A health camp provided consultations” may be supported by verified service records.
- “Patients recovered” must not be claimed from consultation records alone.

Public communication should follow:

**Operational Record → Verification → Indicator/Fact → Approval → Public Communication**

## 9. Overlay tracking

Every overlay item should be recorded in the HSF overlay register with, at minimum:

- Overlay ID
- date/window
- category
- programme/area
- location/context
- related core theme, where natural
- content/activity title
- format
- evidence/source
- evidence status
- safeguarding/consent status
- approval status
- platform
- publication status/link
- performance and learning

Core and overlay reporting remain separate.

## 10. Safeguarding and privacy

Before publication:

- confirm appropriate consent;
- avoid unnecessary names and identifiers;
- do not expose student/patient registers, prescriptions, records, phone numbers or addresses;
- check backgrounds for confidential documents;
- prefer dignified group/activity images where appropriate;
- do not frame children or vulnerable people primarily through hardship or dependency;
- do not present AI-generated people as real HSF participants;
- distinguish observed activity from claimed impact.

**Principle: Human-centred communication without exploitation.**

## 11. 30-day review

After approximately 30 days, management should review:

- core posts completed / 48;
- overlays by category and programme;
- posting frequency and missed/skipped mornings;
- strongest formats;
- reach, engagement, saves/shares where relevant;
- useful audience responses;
- evidence quality;
- safeguarding/privacy issues;
- programme balance;
- staff production burden;
- repetition/overcrowding risk;
- whether the two-window model should continue, change or stop.

Decision options:

**Continue → Adjust → Reduce → Formalize**

A high-reach post is not automatically a high-value post.

**Measure → Interpret → Learn → Adapt**
