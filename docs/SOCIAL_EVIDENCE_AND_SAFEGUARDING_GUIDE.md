# HSF Social Evidence & Safeguarding Guide

**Applies to:** HSF in Action and HSF Knowledge & Awareness

## Evidence chain

**Operational record → Verification → Approved claim → Communication → Learning**

## Claim boundary

**Activity ≠ output ≠ outcome ≠ impact**

A photograph may support that an activity happened. It normally cannot prove longer-term change by itself.

## Evidence labels

Where relevant distinguish:
- Verified
- Observed / documented
- Calculated
- Inferred / estimated
- Pending verification

Never present inferred information as verified.

## Child safeguarding

- obtain appropriate consent;
- avoid unnecessary names or identifiers;
- protect attendance/results/register information;
- do not frame children primarily through poverty or distress;
- prefer dignified learning/activity imagery;
- use group imagery when individual identification adds no communication value.

## Patient/privacy safeguarding

- do not expose diagnoses, prescriptions, registers, phone numbers or addresses;
- do not imply recovery/treatment outcome from consultation imagery;
- use aggregated/anonymized health reporting wherever possible.

## Visual background check

Before release inspect the full frame for:
- registers;
- IDs;
- screens;
- medical documents;
- phone numbers;
- addresses;
- sensitive locations/context;
- people who have not consented.

## A2PHC current service boundary

Communicate verified consultations, health camps and awareness activity. Do not communicate medicine as a standard present service unless specifically verified for that activity.
