# HSF Social Communication — 30-Day Pilot Review Framework

**Pilot period:** September 2026  
**Decision purpose:** Determine whether the two-window operating model should continue, be adjusted, reduced or formalized.

## 1. Review questions

### Core protection
- Were P01–P48 published in order?
- Did overlays displace or delay the 4 PM core stream unnecessarily?
- Were Issue → Resolution pairs kept understandable?

### Overlay quality
- How many overlay posts were published by category?
- How many mornings were intentionally skipped because content did not meet the decision gate?
- Were posts meaningfully different, or did repetitive “today we did…” patterns emerge?

### Programme balance
- Was communication distributed reasonably across E4BL, A2PHC, Climate Action and HSF institutional work?
- Did the most photographically active programme dominate the page beyond its institutional importance?

### Evidence & safeguarding
- Were all public claims traceable?
- Were any claims stronger than the evidence supported?
- Were consent/privacy/background checks documented?
- Were there any safeguarding or privacy incidents or near misses?

### Audience value
- Which posts generated useful comments, saves, shares, enquiries or stakeholder response?
- Which formats created genuine understanding rather than only impressions/reach?

### Production capacity
- Was two-window publishing manageable for staff?
- Did quality, approval or safeguarding checks weaken under frequency pressure?
- Was morning production short and efficient enough to sustain?

## 2. Minimum quantitative review

Report at least:

- Core posts completed / 48
- Total overlay posts
- Overlay posts by category
- Overlay posts by programme/area
- Published vs skipped morning windows
- Safeguarding/privacy incidents
- Posts with complete evidence/source record
- Average/median reach where platform data is available
- Meaningful engagement indicators appropriate to format

## 3. Qualitative review

Record:

- strongest 3 morning posts and why they worked;
- weakest/repetitive 3 posts and why;
- audience questions or feedback worth responding to;
- programme stories that deserve follow-up;
- recurring evidence gaps;
- recurring safeguarding or production risks;
- production practices that should become SOPs.

## 4. Decision matrix

| Decision | When appropriate | Next action |
|---|---|---|
| **Continue pilot** | Learning is positive but evidence period is still short | Continue without changing stable v3.2 |
| **Adjust** | Model works but timing/frequency/categories need refinement | Update pilot note and register controls |
| **Reduce** | Frequency causes repetition, weak evidence or staff burden | Lower morning frequency; keep core protected |
| **Formalize** | Quality, evidence, safeguarding and capacity are consistently acceptable | Approve Social Communication Plan v3.3 and prepare Knowledge Hub v1.5.0 |

## 5. Formalization gate

A stable v3.3 should be approved only if:

- P01–P48 integrity is maintained;
- morning frequency remains opportunity-led;
- evidence and safeguarding controls are reliable;
- staff capacity is adequate;
- programme balance is acceptable;
- management sees clear communication value;
- the plan can be sustained without manufacturing content.
