## 1. Release Purpose

Version 1.5.0 formalizes HSF's communication operating model as one connected institutional system rather than a stand-alone content calendar.

The release connects:

**Programme activity → evidence → verification → safeguarding → communication approval → public communication → performance learning**

It preserves the approved P01–P48 strategic core while establishing a stable morning field/institutional stream and a separate afternoon knowledge/awareness stream.

---

## 2. Stable Communication Architecture

### Morning — HSF in Action

**Normal publication window:** approximately **9:00–10:00 AM**  
**Purpose:** show real HSF activity, people, programme implementation and institutional life.

Approved overlay categories remain:

- `FIELD` — verified programme / field activity
- `MOMENT` — authentic lightweight human or organizational moment
- `EVENT` — substantial event documentation or story
- `OBS` — relevant observance / national / international day
- `HSF` — institutional milestone, publication, partnership or organizational communication

The morning slot is **activity- and relevance-led, not quota-led**. HSF may replace or skip a planned morning item when the activity did not occur, evidence is inadequate, safeguarding is unresolved, the content is repetitive, or silence is more responsible than publication.

### Afternoon — HSF Knowledge & Awareness

**Normal publication window:** approximately **4:00–5:00 PM**  
**Purpose:** publish HSF's planned evidence-led education, health, climate, rights and community-learning content.

The locked sequence remains:

**P01 → P02 → ... → P48**

Each theme remains a two-post pair:

**Issue today → Resolution tomorrow**

Therefore:

**24 themes × 2 consecutive days = 48-day core communication cycle.**

One core post is published per day. The existing **3 Standard + 1 Special Engagement** format rhythm remains unchanged; Special Engagement changes presentation, not post count.

## 5. HSF Activity Communication Bank

The new activity bank uses three evidence layers:

1. **HSF Regular Activity List** — primary planning source for ongoing, recurring, seasonal and as-needed activities;
2. **HSF Detailed Activity Inventory** — supporting institutional evidence/history catalogue;
3. **current verified programme records** — final authority before any activity is represented as current or completed.

Key governance rule:

> **Being listed as an HSF activity does not prove that the activity occurred today.**

Before FIELD, MOMENT or EVENT publication:

**Plan → verify occurrence → verify evidence/media → safeguarding/privacy check → factual/technical review where required → management approval → publish**

Historical, inferred, outdated or reconciliation-needed activity descriptions must not silently become current public claims.

## 9. Evidence and Safeguarding Controls

Permanent claim boundary:

**Activity ≠ output ≠ outcome ≠ impact**

Examples:

- A documented distribution can support the statement that learning materials were distributed.
- It does not by itself prove improvement in attendance, learning achievement or long-term educational outcomes.
- A medical-camp image can document consultation activity.
- It does not prove treatment success, recovery or clinical outcome.

For children, patients and vulnerable people:

- appropriate consent is required;
- unnecessary names and identifiers must be avoided;
- registers, prescriptions, diagnoses, phone numbers, addresses and personal records must not appear in public media;
- dignity and agency take priority over emotional exploitation;
- safer group/activity images should be preferred where individual identification adds no communication value.

---

## 10. A2PHC Communication Boundary

Older/profile-derived activity descriptions may mention prescription or medicine support. For the current communication baseline:

- HSF may communicate **medical consultation and health-awareness activities** when verified;
- medicine must **not** be presented as a standard current A2PHC service unless the specific approved activity record confirms it;
- consultation must not be described as treatment, recovery or clinical outcome.

This prevents historical programme descriptions from silently becoming present-day service claims.

## 15. Cycle-Close Roadmap

At the end of the 48-day cycle:

### Stage 1 — Freeze the operational record
- finalize publication status and links;
- reconcile substitutions/skipped mornings;
- confirm evidence and safeguarding status;
- archive the completed tracker.

### Stage 2 — Communication MEAL review
Analyse:
- reach;
- meaningful engagement;
- shares;
- saves;
- useful comments/responses;
- format performance;
- theme performance;
- programme representation;
- safeguarding/privacy issues;
- staff production burden.

### Stage 3 — Review each of the 24 themes
Classify each:

**Retain / Refresh / Merge / Split / Retire**

### Stage 4 — Compare planned vs actual HSF in Action
Identify:
- activities that consistently produce useful authentic communication;
- activities with weak evidence/documentation;
- programme areas under-represented publicly;
- repeated content that should be reduced.

### Stage 5 — Management decision for Cycle 2
Build the next cycle from:

**performance evidence + programme priorities + verified new evidence + observance calendar + management priorities**

Do not automatically copy Cycle 1.

### Version direction

- If the architecture remains the same and only rules/content improve: **Social Plan v3.4** and an appropriate Knowledge Hub minor release.
- Use **Social Plan v4.0** only if the communication architecture itself materially changes.
