## Management's updated editorial direction

- Retire the previous 48-post Issue → Resolution pilot from the active v4 framework. Do not maintain a numbered P01-P48 archive or tracker as a continuing v4 resource.
- Retain the **Issue → Practical Response** editorial method, without mandatory pairing, sequence, or fixed production quota.

- Require capacities for real case studies, progress/success stories, challenges and responses, field moments and community voices.
- Treat the original KDI 120-topic research bank as **approved editorial ideas only**, not verified claims.
- Review a separate new PSC 120-idea bank. Its idea-level approval has not yet been granted.
