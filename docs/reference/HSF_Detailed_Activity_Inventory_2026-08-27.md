# Human Safety Foundation (HSF)
## Detailed Activity Inventory and Short Descriptions

**Research date:** 27 August 2026  
**Prepared from:** Human Safety Foundation Facebook Page (primary), HSF Profile dated 29 March 2026 (secondary), and HSF website (supporting)  
**Purpose:** Consolidated institutional activity catalogue for reporting, website content, social communication, project documentation, donor/partner briefing, and organizational knowledge management.

---

## 1. Research Method and Source Priority

This inventory follows the requested hierarchy:

1. **Primary source — HSF Facebook Page:** Publicly indexed posts from the Human Safety Foundation Facebook page were used first to identify recent, recurring, and historically documented activities.
2. **Secondary source — HSF Profile (29 March 2026):** The 50-page profile was used to confirm programme structure, activity descriptions, locations, historical continuity, photographs, and selected cumulative figures.
3. **Supporting source — HSF website:** The WordPress website was used to corroborate programme categories, event types, project areas, and older activity pages.

### Facebook access note

Meta restricts unauthenticated direct crawling of Facebook pages and can temporarily block automated page retrieval. For that reason, the Facebook research used **publicly indexed HSF Facebook page/post results** visible through web search, rather than claiming a complete export of every post ever published. The list below is therefore a **detailed evidence-based activity inventory**, not a mathematically complete Facebook archive.

### Source tags used below

- **FB** — HSF Facebook Page / publicly indexed HSF Facebook post
- **P** — HSF Profile, 29 March 2026
- **W** — HSF website / HSF website event or programme page
- **FB+P+W** — corroborated across all three source classes

# 6. Important Source-Reconciliation Notes

## 6.1 Current number of schools

- The **March 2026 Profile** states that HSF historically managed **eight learning centres**, that the Open-Sky centres are closed, and that HSF currently operates **two full-fledged primary schools**.
- Older website wording refers to **five schools** and over 600 learners.
- For current institutional reporting, the **newer 2026 Profile should take precedence** unless HSF management verifies otherwise.

## 6.2 Health-service totals

- The detailed Profile health section reports **171,563 patients receiving free medical advice from March 2022 to December 2025**.
- The Profile achievement page separately states **“1M+” healthcare access annually**.
- These figures are not directly reconcilable from the reviewed material. Do **not** use the 1M+ annual figure in formal reporting without source-data verification.

## 6.3 A2PHC geographic coverage

- Website pages reflect an earlier phase of project expansion and list several districts.
- The 2026 Profile and current Facebook activity confirm later expansion/continuation, including **Narail**.
- Current Facebook recruitment for physicians in **Narail and Manikganj** further supports continued A2PHC operations in those areas.

## 6.4 Emergency-relief total

- The Profile achievement page states **80K+ emergency relief support**, but the reviewed sources do not show enough activity-by-activity evidence to reconstruct this total.
- Keep it as an **unverified cumulative claim** until supported by distribution registers, reports, or annual summaries.

## 6.5 Sustainable Economic Development figures

- The Profile includes annual/cumulative economic-development claims, but detailed project narratives are much thinner than the education and health sections.
- Future institutional documentation should capture project name, location, dates, participant counts, intervention type, and outcome evidence for this sector.

# 11. Conclusion

The available evidence shows that HSF's activity history is broader than a small set of isolated projects. Its work combines **direct service delivery**, **education continuity**, **community health**, **climate resilience**, **women's and girls' wellbeing**, **social protection**, **livelihood-oriented support**, **rights awareness**, **volunteerism**, and **institutional advocacy**.

The strongest and most consistently documented operational pillars are currently:

1. **Education for Better Life (E4BL)**
2. **Access to Primary Health Care (A2PHC)**
3. **Climate Action and resilience activities**
4. **Menstrual hygiene / women and adolescent-girl health**
5. **Seasonal and emergency social-protection support**
6. **Volunteer, partnership, and public-awareness engagement**

For future institutional reporting, HSF would benefit from maintaining one central **Activity Register** containing: activity date, programme, location, activity type, participants/beneficiaries, partner, evidence link, photographs, verified output, responsible staff member, and reporting status.
