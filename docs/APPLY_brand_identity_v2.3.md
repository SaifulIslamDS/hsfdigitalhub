# Apply Record — Brand Identity & Social Design System v2.3

**Effective:** 1 September 2026  
**Previous:** v2.2  
**New:** v2.3  
**Status:** Stable

## Change summary

v2.3 adds formal visual governance for two named communication streams:

### HSF in Action
Authentic-photo-first, lighter graphic treatment, minimal unnecessary text, human presence and strong safeguarding.

### HSF Knowledge & Awareness
Structured designed communication, connected Issue/Resolution presentation, evidence/source treatment and Standard/Special Engagement architecture.

## Preserved

- Evergreen / Trust Blue / Hope Gold / Care Red / Dignity Violet;
- approved logo governance;
- typography rules;
- 16 content families;
- overlay taxonomy;
- safeguarding;
- evidence treatment;
- Album = Documentation / Carousel = Storytelling / Moment = Human Presence.

v2.3 does not certify or regenerate Brand Design Stack v3.1.0 assets.
