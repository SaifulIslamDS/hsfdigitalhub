# Social Communication Ecosystem v4.0: Brand-Production Alignment

**Status:** Approved & Stable alignment guidance  
**Last updated:** 20 October 2026  
**Planning & Compiled by:** Saiful Islam

The Social v4.0 framework is the canonical communication policy. Brand Identity & Design System v2.4 updates editorial mapping without altering the approved HSF official identity, five controlled colour expressions, typography rules, safeguarding safeguards, logo rules or available design-stack assets.

Visual production uses two open-ended streams: HSF in Action and HSF Knowledge & Awareness. The seven reusable content families include authentic field events, documented case studies and community voices, measured progress, challenges/responses, Issue → Practical Response, statistics/knowledge and institutional engagement.

The **KDI 120** and **PSC 120** idea banks are approved for editorial planning but each source, claim, event, participant image, outcome and final visual must be separately verified and management-approved before publication. Never use a hypothetical field scenario as evidence that an activity occurred.

The old fixed-post cycle, old historical post maps and mandatory 3+1 cadence no longer define active publishing or visual production. Issue-and-response pairing remains optional where useful.

Brand Design Stack v3.1.0 remains a **production candidate** until representative Illustrator 2022 artwork has been inspected and certified. Its policy metadata now references Social v4.0 + Brand v2.4.
