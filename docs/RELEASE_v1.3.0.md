# HSF Digital Transformation Knowledge Hub v1.3.0

## Release focus
Evidence-led human stories, case studies and field voices integrated into the existing Social Communication system without changing the 24-theme / 48-post architecture.

## What changed
- Expanded Social Communication guidance for Human Journey, Case Study, Outcome Story, selective Success Story, Field Micro-Story and Voice Story treatments.
- Added “A Day of a Dreamer” as an optional storytelling treatment, not a new theme or extra post.
- Documented HSF Grade 5 / HSF Graduate / Child Sponsorship Initiative (CSI) communication logic.
- Clarified CSI: individual donor → individual CSI student; HSF manages the fund; HSF teachers supervise groups/clusters of CSI students; secondary education occurs at independent third-party schools.
- Added teacher, field supervisor and General Physician / Medical Officer voices as approved story sources where evidence and safeguarding support use.
- Clarified current A2PHC public-service boundary: medical consultation is current; medicine is not currently a standard service and may be considered in future subject to available funding and approved capacity.
- Added health-story evidence distinctions for consultation, advice/prescription, self-reported outcomes and clinically verified outcomes.
- Strengthened Story Evidence Sheet, consent, privacy, safeguarding and contribution-boundary guidance across Social Communication, MEAL and Website Editorial documents.
- Expanded Brand Guide treatment for human journeys/case studies while preserving the sixteen-template system.
- Added light Digital Transformation cross-references for story governance and institutional knowledge.
- Corrected the Netlify deployment guide so all six master documents, including the Impact/MEAL framework, are listed.
- Corrected an obsolete Social Communication sentence that still described the retired split-theme pairing model.

## What did not change
- Six master-document architecture.
- 24 Themes × 2 connected Issue–Resolution posts = 48 posts.
- Every fourth post remains Special Engagement.
- Existing design system and responsive UI.
- Homepage/document filenames and navigation behavior.
- Netlify Edge PIN gate, login/logout handlers, session model, rate limiting, noindex/no-store controls.
- ERP application link patch introduced in v1.2.1.
- ERP Overview itself remains unchanged in this release.

## Security note
No file under `netlify/edge-functions/` and no security/deployment configuration file was modified for v1.3.0.
