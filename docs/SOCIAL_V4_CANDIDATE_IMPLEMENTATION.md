# HSF Social Communication Ecosystem v4.0: Candidate Implementation

**Prepared:** 2026-10-20  
**Status:** Candidate / management review required before replacement of stable policy  
**Stable baseline unchanged:** Hub v1.5.0, Social v3.3, Brand v2.3, Brand Stack v3.1.0 production candidate, Digital System Contract v1.0.0.

## Approved decisions integrated

- Fixed 48-post pilot retired **from the active v4 candidate ecosystem**; earlier stable v3.3 remains unmodified as historical policy.
- Issue → Resolution approach retained as reusable and expandable editorial family. The 48-post archive map and old cycle tracker are removed from active v4 communication assets.
- Two public streams retained: **HSF in Action** and **HSF Knowledge & Awareness**.
- Mandatory ongoing editorial capacities: authentic FIELD cases, human experiences, progress/success, challenge/response and lessons learned.
- Approved **120 KDI topic ideas** remain unchanged, while **120 PSC draft ideas** are added as a separate second bank. None of the 240 ideas are approved posts or verified claims.
- Flexible content frequency, no fixed numbering ceiling, no required daily morning/afternoon slot, no compulsory two-day issue/response pairing or 3+1 format quota.
- Programme baselines, approved brand identity and MEAL/ERP data governance retained as-is.

## Source-of-truth and rights boundaries

Keep private records in operational systems, approved public claim records in the Approved Information Layer, and only authorized public material in website/social systems. Publication requires a source page/table and reference period, or authenticated occurrence record, plus technical and safeguarding review appropriate to risk and *management approval of final copy and image*. No invented impact.

## Change from stable v3.3

Stable v3.3 **requires** daily P01–P48 and uses legacy Core/Overlay mechanics. Candidate v4 provides two non-compulsory streams whose content is driven by genuine programme events and verified knowledge. Categories are independent: programme, stream, family, editorial domain, medium and platform.

## Historical release compatibility

Previous v3.3 source files are unchanged for historical audit, but no numbered 48-post planning/heritage system appears in the active v4 candidate. `social_communication_plan.html` remains stable until formal promotion. Brand Guide remains v2.3, and its legacy references remain truthful about the *stable* system. Brand Stack metadata is deliberately not deceptively certified as a new v4 production release.
