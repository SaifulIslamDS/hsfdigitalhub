# HSF Digital Knowledge Hub v1.6.0 Candidate

**Prepared:** 2026-10-20  
**Status:** Candidate only; **not a stable release**, deployed policy change or management approval of individual source findings.  
**Previous / current stable:** Knowledge Hub v1.5.0, Social v3.3, Brand v2.3, Brand Stack v3.1.0 production candidate.

## Feature scope

Social Communication Ecosystem v4.0 candidate implements open-ended two-stream publishing, seven families, eight independent editorial domains, retained Issue → Resolution library, mandated support for authentic field cases/progress/challenges, and a 120-idea approved but unverified KDI research bank.

Candidate controls include source-first publishing and evidence statuses, independent approval, risk-sensitive technical/safeguarding review, design QA, blank metadata schema for future ERP handoff, and separate analytics/feedback lifecycle.

## Explicit exclusions

No change to approved brand visual identity, programme statuses, ERP or MEAL baselines, Netlify PIN or security configuration, original 48 posts, existing 48-day tracker, or recorded stable v1.5.0 documents. No public statistics are approved or published by the KDI idea bank.

## Tests required

`python scripts/validate_hub.py` and `python scripts/validate_social_v4.py`, followed by actual browser/Netlify access and Illustrator design QA where used.

### Second idea bank revision (20 October 2026)
The v4 candidate now includes a separate **PSC 120-idea draft bank** in addition to approved KDI 120 research ideas. The P01–P48 legacy archive map and active cycle tracker were retired from the v4 ecosystem. The historical Social v3.3 source remains unmodified pending v4 approval.
