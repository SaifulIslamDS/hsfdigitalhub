## Purpose and scope

Two reusable, optional editorial series help communicate real classroom practice and offer useful educational activities. They are **subseries**, not new streams or eighth/ninth content families. They do not impose daily publishing quotas or mandatory two-post cycles. Both sit within the current 500-idea catalogue. These labels are controlled through `communication/editorial_series.json`; the authoritative JSON and XLSX idea banks remain synchronized.

| Subseries | Stream / bank | Intended output | Minimum pre-publication evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Inside Our Classroom** | HSF in Action / PSC | Real lesson moments, teacher methods, playful participation, activities | Dated actual session, centre confirmation, educational context, permission and safeguarding check, exact-copy approval |
| **Learn with E4BL** | HSF Knowledge & Awareness / KDI | Simple learning tips, vocabulary, reading, counting, parent-assisted practice | Clear age/grade scope, teacher-reviewed subject accuracy, correct examples, suitable learning references, exact-copy approval |

## Inside Our Classroom: evidence workflow

1. Identify an **actual** class session and teacher-led activity. Confirm the occurrence in approved field records; avoid reconstructing fictional student actions.
2. Describe what was taught and how learners participated. Focus on the method, moment and teacher's observation without assigning names or identifiable personal facts to children.
3. Confirm safeguarding and media permissions separately for any recognizable child, voice, photo or video. Children's personal details, full names, individual assessments and consent forms stay outside this public GitHub repository.
4. The education/field focal person verifies lesson content and actual activity. Editorial team reviews language, management approves the final text and media before publication.
5. Preserve the approved public URL, date, content asset ID, cited idea ID, series tag, responsible owner and evidence-record reference **in the controlled operational content register**, not a populated beneficiary dataset in this repository.

**Outcome caution:** A joyful lesson does not alone demonstrate improvement in English proficiency, attendance, literacy or measurable learning outcome.

## Learn with E4BL: practical learning tip workflow

1. Define **one skill** and the appropriate grade/age group (e.g., associate a dog with “woof” and a cat with “meow”).
2. Use accurate, respectful, understandable English or Bangla; teacher/subject reviewer checks content and any curriculum implications.
3. Make the activity actionable (e.g., *say the animal name, listen to or imitate its sound, then match picture and word*). Offer a simple family practice option without suggesting households are required to purchase resources.
4. Do not claim “research proves this method increases scores” without a verified specific study that supports that statement, with limitations.
5. Obtain approval for the exact caption, translation, graphics, illustrations and publication date before posting.

### Optional example (draft, not a published or management-approved post)

**Can You Match These Animal Sounds? 🐶🐱**  
Dog → Woof! | Cat → Meow! | Cow → Moo! | Duck → Quack!  
**Try it:** Say each animal's name, then make its sound together. Ask a parent, teacher or friend to switch the order. This is a practice suggestion, not evidence of a measurable learning gain.

## Governance boundary

The Digital Knowledge Hub is an internally gated web site, **but its GitHub source repository can be public**. Do not commit actual student lists, images requiring restricted consent, individual assessment marks, sensitive guardian concerns, personnel files, unpublished monitoring data or completed confidential operational registers. The blank register templates are intentionally public-safe.

**Source of truth:** Operational ERP/approved field registers → verification/MEAL → decision → exact-output approval → publication. Content idea status and software release status do not grant permission to publish.
