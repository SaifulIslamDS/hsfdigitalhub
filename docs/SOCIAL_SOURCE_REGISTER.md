## Evidence rule

**Source presence does not equal publication approval.**

Communication should follow:

**Source → Verification → Claim classification → Safeguarding/technical review → Management approval → Publication**

## A2PHC reconciliation note

The preserved activity references include older/profile-derived descriptions of prescription/medicine support. Under the current communication baseline, HSF should communicate medical consultation and verified health-awareness activity; medicine must not be presented as a standard current service unless the specific approved activity record confirms it.
