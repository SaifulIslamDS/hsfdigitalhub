Foundation & Governance

Executive Summary

HSF ERP is planned as the central digital operating system of Human Safety Foundation.

Open detailed reference
HSF ERP is planned as the central digital operating system of Human Safety Foundation.

Its purpose is to connect HSF's planning, people, projects, funds, activities, approvals, documents, achievements, and reports through one controlled platform.

Many HSF activities are still managed through Excel, Google Forms, paper files, email, WhatsApp, bank statements, phone calls and personal follow-up. These tools work, but they also create common problems:

  • The same information may be entered more than once.
  • Different people may keep different versions of the same file.
  • Management may not always see the latest status immediately.
  • Approval history may remain in email, WhatsApp, or verbal communication.
  • Project results and financial expenditure may not be connected clearly.
  • Monthly and annual reports require repeated manual work.
  • Important organizational knowledge may remain with individual staff members.

HSF ERP will not remove human responsibility. It will make responsibility clearer.

The system will help HSF answer important management questions:

  • What did we plan?
How much was spent?
  • Who was responsible?
Which donor or fund supported the work?
  • What was the approved target?
Which requests are waiting for approval?
  • What work was completed?
Which reports are late?
  • Who received the service?
What problems require management attention?
  • What evidence was submitted?
What was achieved compared with the plan?
  • How much money was approved?

Human Safety Foundation is a non-governmental, non-political, and non-profit organization working for vulnerable, marginalized, hard-to-reach, and climate-affected people.

Human Safety Foundation is a non-governmental, non-political, and non-profit organization working for vulnerable, marginalized, hard-to-reach, and climate-affected people.

HSF's work includes education, primary healthcare, climate action, women empowerment, community support, donor-funded activities, field operations, staff management, and central-office administration.

HSF already has working processes. The problem is not that there is no process. The problem is that the processes are separated across many files and communication channels.

HSF ERP will convert these separate processes into one connected institutional system.

A Single Source of Truth

A single source of truth means that HSF will have one trusted system for official information.

Open detailed reference
A single source of truth means that HSF will have one trusted system for official information.

It does not mean every user can see everything. It means:

  • there is one approved version of a record
  • the latest status is clear
  • the responsible person is known
  • changes are recorded
  • approvals are visible
  • reports use the same verified data
  • documents remain linked to the correct transaction or activity.

Examples

  • One employee record supports HR, attendance, payroll, training, and project assignment.
  • One student record supports enrolment, attendance, results, material distribution, graduate tracking,
  • and sponsorship.
  • One patient record can have many visits without creating a new patient every time.
  • One approved activity connects target, staff work, beneficiaries, cost, evidence, and result.
  • One transaction appears correctly in the project report, donor report, cash book, ledger, and annual
  • report.

Foundation & Governance

How the Complete System Will Work

Management approves strategic direction, project plans, annual targets, staffing, training, procurement, budgets, and expected reports.

Open detailed reference

Stage 1: Plan

Management approves strategic direction, project plans, annual targets, staffing, training, procurement, budgets, and expected reports.

Stage 2: Assign

Each activity is assigned to a project, location, responsible person, supervisor, target, deadline, indicator, and budget or funding source.

Stage 3: Request

Field offices and responsible staff submit operational needs.

Examples

  • Head Teacher submits the next month's school needs.
  • Medical Supervisor submits the next month's camp and medicine needs.
  • Department head submits office or training needs.

Stage 4: Review and approve

The Project Coordinator reviews and consolidates needs. Finance checks budget, account head, funding source, previous expenditure, and available balance. Authorized management approves, returns, or rejects.

Stage 5: Implement

Funds, materials, services, and responsibilities move to the field or department. Staff record activities, attendance, beneficiaries, service delivery, challenges, evidence, and expenditure.

Stage 6: Report achievement

Individual staff submit daily or monthly reports. Supervisors review. Project Coordinators consolidate. Management sees project performance.

Stage 7: Account for funds

Bills, vouchers, receipts, bank entries, IOUs, and other documents are submitted and verified. Finance posts accounting records and completes reconciliation.

Stage 8: Close and report

The system checks missing items. When all important requirements are complete, the month is closed. Verified information supports management, project, donor, financial, audit, and annual reports.

Common Record and Approval States

Users should save work before submission.

Open detailed reference
Users should save work before submission.

A common record lifecycle may be

Draft
↓
Submitted
↓
Under Review
↓
Returned for Correction
↓
Resubmitted
↓
Recommended
↓
Approved
↓
In Progress
↓
Completed
↓
Verified
↓
Closed

Other possible states include Rejected, Cancelled, On Hold, Partially Approved, Partially Completed, Partially Disbursed, Adjusted, and Reopened.

It is recommended to state Meaning

Draft The user is still preparing the record.

Submitted The user has formally sent it for review.

Under Review A reviewer is checking it.

Returned Corrections or more information are required.

Recommended A reviewer supports approval, but final approval is pending.

Approved The authorized person has approved it.

In Progress Implementation has started.

Completed The activity or task is finished.

Verified Evidence and result have been checked.

Closed Normal changes are no longer allowed.

Reopened An authorized person reopened the record with a reason.

Every status change should record the user, date, comment, previous status, and new status.

Recommended Implementation Approach

HSF ERP should be introduced in phases.

Open detailed reference
HSF ERP should be introduced in phases. Building everything at one time would create high risk.

Phase 1: Platform and access

Login, users, roles, projects, locations, permissions, audit log, notifications, and document foundation.

Phase 2: Core master data

Projects, locations, fiscal year, accounts, funds, donors, activities, units, vendors, and employees.

Phase 3: Planning and staff reporting

Annual plan, individual work plan, daily activity, monthly achievement, project management report, and project achievement report.

Phase 4: Field requisition and finance control

Field requisition, MFR, additional MFR, approval, disbursement, and PR.

Phase 5: Expense and accounting

IOU, bill, voucher, cash, bank, MFS, journal, reconciliation, and closing.

Phase 6: HR and payroll

Employee, recruitment, attendance, leave, training, and payroll.

Phase 7: E4BL

Student, enrolment, attendance, results, teacher reporting, and graduate support.

Phase 8: A2PHC

Camp, patient, visit, doctor input, prescription, referral, and medicine.

Phase 9: Meetings, training, donor, and advanced reporting

Meetings, resolutions, notices, training library, donor and grant, dashboards, and exports.

Recommended pilot

It is recommended to start with one project, one location, selected users, one month, one approval cycle, one finance cycle, and one management review. Improve the system after the pilot before wider rollout.

Conclusion

HSF ERP is not simply a new software application.

Open detailed reference
HSF ERP is not simply a new software application. It is a plan to create a stronger institutional operating system for Human Safety Foundation.

It will connect planning, people, projects, service delivery, beneficiaries, finance, procurement, HR, meetings, training, documents, evidence, achievements, decisions, and reports.

The most important idea is

  • HSF should connect every important result with the related
  • plan, responsible person, approved fund, activity, evidence,
  • expenditure, and report.

  • a daily working platform for staff
  • a review platform for supervisors
  • a control platform for finance
  • a decision platform for management
  • an accountability platform for donors and auditors; and
  • an institutional memory for the future.

HSF ERP will become the single, reliable, secure, and integrated source of truth for Human Safety Foundation.
