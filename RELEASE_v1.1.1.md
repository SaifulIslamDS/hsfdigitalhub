# HSF Digital Transformation Knowledge Hub v1.1.1

## Simplified 24-Theme / 48-Post Social Communication Plan

The Social Communication Plan is simplified to **24 Themes × 2 connected posts = 48 posts**. Each theme contains one Issue post and one Resolution post. No theme-to-theme mapping such as `Theme 01 + Theme 13` is used.

The 3+1 rhythm controls presentation format across the 48-post sequence: three standard-format posts followed by one Special Engagement post. Special Engagement remains part of the 48 posts and does not create an extra post.

The first 48 posts form the first content cycle. The next cycle is intended to be developed after publication, measurement and learning.

The Brand Identity Master Guide is synchronized with the simplified Issue–Resolution architecture. PIN security, Netlify Edge Functions, noindex controls, six-document structure, safeguarding and evidence governance remain unchanged.
