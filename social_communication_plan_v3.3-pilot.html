4:00 PM is reserved for the locked P01–P48 sequence. Overlay communication must not replace, renumber or displace the core series.

3. Morning Stream Rule

The morning window is opportunity-led, not quota-led. HSF may publish one morning overlay when meaningful, safe and evidence-supported content exists. A morning post should be skipped when the material is repetitive, weakly evidenced, unsafe, or not useful enough to justify publication.

MOMENT
Most frequent lightweight format. One authentic image + 2–4 short sentences. No forced statistic or CTA.
FIELD
Meaningful programme activity. Usually 2–5 photos + concise narrative.
EVENT
Substantial event only. Album = Documentation. Carousel = Storytelling.
OBS / HSF
Date- or milestone-driven. Publish only where relevance is genuine.

4. Editorial Decision Gate

Is it real? → Is it useful? → Is it safe? → Is it sufficiently different from recent posts? → Is evidence available? → Is publication better than silence?

If an important answer is “no”, hold or skip the morning post.

5. Evidence & Safeguarding

  • Activity ≠ outcome. Do not strengthen field facts into outcome or impact claims without evidence.
  • Distinguish verified, observed/documented, calculated, inferred/estimated and pending-verification information.
  • For children, patients and vulnerable people, confirm appropriate consent and protect dignity and privacy.
  • Do not expose registers, prescriptions, medical records, phone numbers, addresses or sensitive documents.
  • Use safer group/activity images where individual identification adds no legitimate communication value.

6. Overlay Register & Measurement

Every overlay post should be recorded in communication/registers/social-overlay-register.xlsx. Core and overlay performance must remain separate.

At approximately 30 days, management should review programme balance, reach and meaningful engagement, content quality, evidence integrity, safeguarding, staff production burden, audience fatigue and whether the two-window model should Continue → Adjust → Reduce → Formalize.

7. Brand Alignment

Brand Identity & Social Design System v2.2 remains sufficient for this pilot. No new palette or brand stream is created. Continue using Evergreen, Trust Blue, Hope Gold, Care Red and Dignity Violet according to existing rules.

Album = Documentation · Carousel = Storytelling · Moment = Human Presence
